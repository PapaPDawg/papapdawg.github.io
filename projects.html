Graveyard Shift

A cozy, melancholy relationship sim about working the night shift at a cemetery: tending the grounds, getting to know the ghosts who linger there, and the quiet drive home with Grandma.

Built in Unreal Engine 5 with a data-driven dialogue system, randomized daily chores, ghost affinity with persistence, and a multi-day loop across levels.

Status: Active development (vertical slice). Working title.
